## 1. Key Concepts Learned (Passive, No Lab Today)
* **Firewalls, ground-up:** Watched "What is a Firewall?" (PowerCert Animated Videos) — a firewall acts as a barrier between a trusted internal network and the untrusted internet, filtering traffic in or out.
* **Access Control Lists (ACLs):** The customizable rules a firewall actually uses to decide what's allowed in or kept out — based on criteria like IP address, domain name, protocol, port, or program.
* **Software vs. network firewalls:** Software firewalls protect a single device (installed on the OS); network firewalls (hardware/combined) protect an entire network at its edge.
